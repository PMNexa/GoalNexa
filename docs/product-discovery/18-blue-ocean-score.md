## Top 5 by average score

1. **Migrate-from-Notion Wizard** (7.17) — highest score overall, driven by real, well-evidenced New Demand ([04 — Current Solutions](04-current-solutions.md)'s documented Notion/Sheets template economy) and strong ERRC fit ([09](09-errc-grid.md) Raise column).
2. **One Instance, Two Layers** (6.67, tied) — highest Differentiation score in the top 5 (9/10) — genuinely nothing like it in [05 — Competitor Map](05-competitor-map.md) — but weak Market Potential (4/10), reflecting how narrow the solopreneur/tiny-team segment is.
2. **Solopreneur Tier** (6.67, tied) — closely related to #2 above (same underlying segment, different packaging framing); scores nearly identically.
4. **Bus-Factor Dashboard** (6.50, tied) — near-perfect Feasibility (9/10) and Competitive Resistance (9/10), but the lowest New Demand score of the top 5 (4/10) — this is a trust-reinforcing feature, not a standalone demand driver.
4. **Quantified Team (auto OKRs)** (6.50, tied) — high Customer Value and Differentiation (8/8), dragged down by the lowest Feasibility of the top 5 (4/10) — real engineering cost to build multiple integrations.

## Honest caveats on these scores

- **SSO-Native (#8) and Notify-via-Apprise (#9) score lower than their [14](14-blue-ocean-concepts.md) write-ups implied**, because [05 — Competitor Map](05-competitor-map.md) already documents HabitSync shipping both — their Differentiation and Competitive Resistance scores were marked down accordingly. This is a useful check on ideation-stage overclaiming.
- **Market Potential is the weakest-evidenced dimension across the board** — every score here is a reasoned estimate, not a sized market. None of docs 01-17 produced a usable bottom-up addressable-market number for any single concept; this is the biggest open gap heading into [19 — Strategic Fit](19-strategic-fit.md) and [21 — Willingness to Pay](21-willingness-to-pay.md).
- Concepts scoring high on Differentiation and Competitive Resistance but low on Market Potential (Federated Pods, Ambient Display, Verified Referee — all 9/9 on Differentiation/Resistance but 2-3 on Market Potential) are a recognizable Blue-Ocean trap: genuinely uncontested space that may be uncontested *because* the addressable population is too small to matter, not because it's undiscovered. [19](19-strategic-fit.md) and [20 — Competition Test](20-competition-test.md) examine this distinction further.
