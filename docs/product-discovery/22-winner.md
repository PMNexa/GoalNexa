# 22 — Winner: Quantified Team

**Selected concept:** #15 from [14 — Blue Ocean Concepts](14-blue-ocean-concepts.md) — a self-hosted OKR tool whose key results update automatically from data teams already generate in their existing self-hosted stack (Gitea/GitLab commit activity, Vikunja/Plane task completion), priced flat-rate regardless of headcount.

## Why this one, not the highest-scoring concept

[18 — Blue Ocean Score](18-blue-ocean-score.md) ranked Migrate-from-Notion Wizard highest (7.17 average) and Quantified Team tied for 4th (6.50). This selection deliberately overrides the raw ranking, because [21 — Willingness to Pay](21-willingness-to-pay.md) found Quantified Team is the **only one of 14 surviving concepts with a demonstrable existing budget line to redirect** — current per-seat OKR SaaS spend ($9-11/user/mo at Perdoo/Quantive, up to $108-2,025/mo at Weekdone depending on team size, [05](05-competitor-map.md)). Migrate-from-Notion Wizard scored higher on novelty and addressable population size, but [21](21-willingness-to-pay.md) established it has no standalone monetization path — it's a conversion feature, not a product. Per PM principle 13 ("continuously test whether customers have a problem they are willing to pay to solve") and 16 ("do not recommend building an MVP simply because the idea sounds interesting"), willingness-to-pay evidence outranks ideation-stage novelty here.

## Why it creates new demand

- **Converts Tier 1 noncustomers** ([10 — Noncustomers](10-noncustomers.md)) — teams currently paying for Weekdone/Perdoo/Quantive, or Viva Goals refugees who migrated SaaS-to-SaaS rather than self-hosting ([06 — Pressure Test](06-pressure-test.md)) because no self-hosted alternative offered comparable convenience until Operately, and even Operately still requires manual updates.
- **Converts a slice of Tier 3 noncustomers** — self-hosting technical teams already running Gitea/Vikunja/Plane ([01 — Market Map](01-market-map.md)) who never adopted any OKR tool, formal or informal, because the admin overhead of manual updates outweighed the perceived benefit. This trigger is an ASSUMPTION, not a direct quote ([19 — Strategic Fit](19-strategic-fit.md)) — the highest-priority thing to validate, not a settled fact.
- **Changes the competed-on factor**, per [08 — Strategy Canvas](08-strategy-canvas.md) and [09 — ERRC Grid](09-errc-grid.md): every competitor found, including Operately, competes on manual-input reporting depth. Nothing in [05 — Competitor Map](05-competitor-map.md) automates key-result computation. This is a genuine Create-column move, not a Raise-column improvement on an existing factor.

## Why customers would switch from existing alternatives

| Current alternative | Why they'd switch |
|---|---|
| Weekdone / Perdoo / Quantive (per-seat SaaS) | Flat-rate pricing removes the factor ranked HIGH in [07 — Industry Factors](07-industry-factors.md) that punishes team growth; automation removes the manual-update labor cost entirely, not just the software cost |
| Operately (self-hosted, flat-rate, manual updates) | The one factor Operately hasn't addressed — [05](05-competitor-map.md) confirms no automation in its feature set. This product doesn't out-compete Operately on trust or pricing (Operately already won those); it competes on a factor Operately hasn't touched |
| No tool at all (Slack/email/meetings) | Removes the "who has time to update this" objection that plausibly keeps very small technical teams from adopting any formal OKR process — untested, flagged in [10 — Noncustomers](10-noncustomers.md) |
| Spreadsheets (personal habit tracking, adjacent but not this product's target) | Not this product's target customer — this concept is scoped to the team/OKR segment specifically, not the individual habit-tracking segment; see [12 — Strategic Groups](12-strategic-groups.md) for why those remain separate opportunities |

## What this pick is not

RECOMMENDATION, stated plainly: this is the strongest concept **this desk research can support**, not a validated product-market fit. [06 — Pressure Test](06-pressure-test.md) already established the team-OKR wedge as "cautiously open," not proven — a smaller player (Operately) has validated the shape works, not that a second, automation-differentiated entrant will. [25 — MVP](25-mvp.md) and [26 — Experiment](26-experiment.md) exist specifically because this selection still needs direct validation before any real engineering investment.
