# 31 — Final Decision: PIVOT

After 30 documents of market research, customer research, Blue Ocean strategy, ideation, validation scoring, and adversarial stress-testing, the recommendation for **Quantified Team** ([22 — Winner](22-winner.md)) as currently scoped is **PIVOT — do not build; run the validation this research is designed to demand, and re-scope around what survives it.**

This is not a KILL: real, evidenced positive signal exists (existing redirectable budget, a working existence proof in Operately, a large and growing self-hosting community). It is not a GO: the concept's central pain is unquoted by any real customer across this entire research effort, its core differentiator turns out to be structurally copyable by SaaS incumbents without self-hosting at all ([27 — Red Team](27-red-team.md)), and its moat is thin on five of seven dimensions ([29 — Moat](29-moat.md)).

## Five strongest reasons for this decision

1. **The core pain has zero direct customer evidence.** [19 — Strategic Fit](19-strategic-fit.md) and [23 — Value Proposition](23-value-proposition.md) both flag it, and nothing in [24](24-business-model.md) through [30](30-market-timing.md) found any first-person quote confirming manual OKR-update overhead is a real, felt burden. A business built on an unquoted assumption, in a research process whose own governing principle is "continuously test whether customers have a problem they are willing to pay to solve," cannot honestly clear a GO bar yet.

2. **The differentiator is orthogonal to the product's category.** [27 — Red Team](27-red-team.md)'s sharpest finding: automated Git/PM-activity-to-key-result mapping doesn't require self-hosting. Any SaaS incumbent (Weekdone, Perdoo, Quantive) or the strongest self-hosted incumbent (Operately, with a real distribution head start) could add this feature without adopting this product's core constraint. A wedge that can be copied by better-resourced players without adopting your hardest tradeoff is not a wedge worth building a company around as currently framed.

3. **The moat is thin on five of seven dimensions.** [29 — Moat](29-moat.md) found weak-to-absent data, technology, brand, network-effect, and switching-cost advantages — the only real advantages identified (ecosystem distribution, lean cost structure) support a durable small business, not defensibility against a well-resourced competitor deciding to compete directly.

4. **Market timing is favorable for the category, not clearly favorable for this specific trigger.** [30 — Market Timing](30-market-timing.md) found the strongest observed real-world evidence of buyer urgency — actual Microsoft Viva Goals refugees — moved to other SaaS vendors, not self-hosting, directly contradicting the "vendor risk drives self-hosting adoption" narrative this concept partly depends on.

5. **The evidence that does hold up is real and shouldn't be discarded.** [21 — Willingness to Pay](21-willingness-to-pay.md)'s finding that existing per-seat OKR SaaS budget is real, current, and redirectable is not refuted by anything in [27](27-red-team.md)-[30](30-market-timing.md) — it's a genuine asset this research surfaced that a KILL verdict would waste. Operately's real, growing traction ([06 — Pressure Test](06-pressure-test.md)) still proves the underlying self-hosted-plus-flat-rate shape works in market. This is exactly the situation [22 — Winner](22-winner.md) already anticipated citing [06](06-pressure-test.md)'s own history: a mixed signal calls for a defined next test, not an extreme verdict in either direction.

## What PIVOT means concretely

- **Do not build the automation engine as scoped.** [25 — MVP](25-mvp.md)'s Stage 0 concierge test and [26 — Experiment](26-experiment.md)'s 30-day plan remain the correct next step, unchanged by this final analysis — but the Week 3 pricing conversation should now explicitly probe the [27 — Red Team](27-red-team.md) objection directly: ask design partners whether they'd still choose this over a hypothetical "Weekdone with a GitHub integration" — if most say no, the differentiation problem is confirmed, not just theorized.
- **Re-scope the value proposition around what a SaaS incumbent genuinely can't copy.** Per [29 — Moat](29-moat.md), that's ecosystem-native distribution ([13 — Complementary Products](13-complementary-products.md): Homepage, Authelia/Authentik, Apprise integration) and the self-hosted trust/ownership factor itself ([07 — Industry Factors](07-industry-factors.md) factor #1) — not the automation mechanism alone. Automation may still belong in the product, but it should be pitched as a convenience layered on top of an ownership-and-ecosystem pitch, not as the primary reason to switch.
- **Keep the door open to the runner-up concepts.** [18 — Blue Ocean Score](18-blue-ocean-score.md)'s next-ranked ideas (Bus-Factor Dashboard, Solopreneur Tier) remain available if the 30-day experiment produces a KILL-level signal on Quantified Team specifically — a PIVOT on this concept is not a PIVOT on the entire self-hosted-goal-tracking opportunity.

## Evidence still missing

- A single direct, unprompted customer quote confirming manual OKR-update overhead as a real, costly pain — the most important gap, closeable within [26 — Experiment](26-experiment.md)'s first two weeks.
- Direct evidence of whether SaaS incumbents would actually bother building a comparable automation feature — currently a reasoned inference from [27 — Red Team](27-red-team.md), not observed competitive behavior.
- A bottom-up, category-specific addressable-market size for self-hosted team-OKR tooling — no document in this 30-part research effort produced one; every market-size figure found was either a broader OKR-SaaS estimate or an adjacent self-hosting-market estimate, never this category specifically.
- Direct validation (or disconfirmation) of the regulated-industry hedge segment flagged repeatedly since [06 — Pressure Test](06-pressure-test.md) and never directly tested.
- Real-world evidence of the actual conversion rate from free self-hosted to paid cloud in this specific category — [24 — Business Model](24-business-model.md)'s revenue-potential figures are all benchmarked from adjacent categories (Plausible, Operately's pricing structure without disclosed revenue), not observed in this category itself.

**Bottom line:** this research earned a specific, falsifiable next experiment — not a build decision, and not a shutdown. That is the intended end state of a rigorous discovery process, not an unsatisfying non-answer.
